# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report vulnerabilities through [GitHub private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/Lore-Hex/llm-trustedrouter/security/advisories/new).
Reports are visible only to maintainers while they are investigated.
Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or include credentials,
personal data, or private user conversations in a report.

Include the affected version or commit, reproduction steps, and expected impact.
A partial report sent early is useful.

## Response

We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours and assess severity and affected
components within five business days. We will notify you when a fix ships and
credit you in an advisory unless you prefer otherwise. There is no bug bounty
programme.

## Scope

This policy covers this repository. Report vulnerabilities in the hosted
TrustedRouter service through [its private reporting channel](https://github.com/Lore-Hex/quill-router/security/advisories/new).

## Safe harbour

We will not pursue or support legal action against anyone who reports a
vulnerability in good faith, avoids privacy violations and service degradation,
and gives us reasonable time to respond before disclosing.
